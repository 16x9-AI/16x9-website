Asure Software signed a $115 million credit facility with MUFG as agent. Asure drew all of its $85.0 million term loan on October 15, 2026.

On October 15, 2026, Asure Software, Inc. entered into a credit agreement with MUFG Bank, Ltd. as administrative agent, with the lenders party to the agreement. Certain of its subsidiaries act as guarantors. The company may borrow up to $115 million under a senior credit facility, comprising an $85.0 million term loan facility, all of which was drawn on October 15, 2026.
