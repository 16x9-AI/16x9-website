Onar Holding bought Advertise Purple for up to $27,825,000. Only $12.825 million arrives in cash at closing.

Onar Holding Corp completed its purchase of Advertise Purple, a Santa Monica marketing agency, on September 30, 2026. Total consideration for the acquisition is up to $27,825,000, assuming all earnout thresholds are met.

Cash at closing: $12.825 million, subject to post-closing adjustments. Seller note: $7.0 million, an unsecured promissory note from the buyer to the seller. Earnout payments: up to $8.0 million, paid if the earnout thresholds are met.

Onar Holding Corp 8-K, 30 September 2026
