## What 16x9 is

An AI studio that builds agentic companies with operators who already know the trade. The operator brings domain expertise, relationships and distribution. 16x9 brings forward-deployed engineers and operators, and Mycel, the orchestration system it built, to move most of the work onto agents. The company ends up lean and profitable. 16x9 partners for equity, a revenue share, or another joint-venture structure, then layers in strategic outcomes (roll-ups, a sale at a better multiple) through a partner desk.

## Who the site serves

- **Owners of established companies** that run on outdated systems and hand back-office work to BPOs or outsourced IT. Examples (Manny's primer, not a closed list): a $10M manufacturer, medical practices, accounting firms, engineering firms, insurance carriers and brokers, mortgage lenders, real estate brokerages, and any business that buys from MSPs or MSSPs. The pitch is margin and growth, never a presumed sale.
- **Founders and operators** with subject-matter expertise who want to lead a new or existing company on growth, product and customer acquisition, with 16x9 running the machine. A Discord community of founders, operators and owners is planned (URL not yet provided).
- **Holding companies, PE and roll-ups** that want one agent operating layer across several companies.
- **Business brokers and advisers**, as a channel: they see the companies first.
- **Builders** who want to work on the platform.

## Offers (shape only, no prices on the site)

Transferred from Frontier Desk's operating work. Exits, lending, data licensing and capital placement stay with Frontier Desk and are referred out, unnamed.

1. Opportunity assessment: fixed fee, about two weeks, a baseline per process and changes ranked by margin. The company keeps it.
2. Fixed-scope build: first one to three automations in live work, midway gate to continue, change or stop.
3. Forward-deployed team: engineers and operators inside the company on a monthly retainer.
4. Operate: 16x9 takes on the back office and is paid from the profit it adds (profit share). Draft offer in FD; terms not ratified.
5. Partner: a new company or joint venture with an operator, for equity or a revenue share.
